## html-ppt-skill

- **Source**: https://github.com/lewislulu/html-ppt-skill
- **Copyright**: Copyright (c) 2026 lewis <sudolewis@gmail.com>
- **License**: MIT
- **Date introduced**: 2026-09-17
- **Scope of adaptation** (all rewritten to fit this skill's single-page static rendering and audio-visual sync system):
  - `slides/tokens.css` generated by `scripts/init-project.mjs`:
    - the color tokens of 10 named themes (`minimal-white` / `swiss-grid` / `corporate-clean` / `editorial-serif` /
      `magazine-bold` / `tokyo-night` / `catppuccin-mocha` / `nord` / `xiaohongshu-white` / `soft-pastel`)
      — the variable names are mapped to this skill's naming (`--surface`→`--panel`, `--text-1/2/3`→`--fg/--muted/--fg-3`,
      `--border`→`--line`, and so on), and the external fonts (Playfair Display / Space Grotesk and others) were replaced with local font stacks
    - the image-frame primitives `.img-frame` / `.contain` / `.fill` / `.img-scrim` / `.img-cap` / `.img-tag` and their
      `--img-ratio` / `--img-pos` variables
    - the keyframes of some entrance animations (`fx-blur` `fx-rise` `fx-pop` `fx-spotlight` `fx-ripple` `fx-glitch`
      `fx-shimmer` `fx-kenburns` `fx-stagger`) — the trigger mechanism was changed from "JS re-triggering on slide change" to
      "`animation-delay: var(--tN)` absolute time" so it works with frame-stepping rendering
    - the typographic roles and container classes `.kicker` / `.eyebrow` / `.lede` / `.card` (with three variants) / `.pill` / `.divider-accent`
  - Some layout skeletons in `references/authoring.md` were rewritten from the versions of the same name under its `templates/single-page/`
  - The image-frame usage rules in `references/image-sources.md` follow the design intent of its `.img-frame`

**Not adopted** (incompatible with its design or carrying extra dependencies): `runtime.js` (keyboard navigation / presenter mode / overview),
the 20 canvas effects under `assets/animations/fx/` (rAF-driven, cannot be frame-seeked),
`fonts.css` and every external Google Fonts link, the Chart.js / highlight.js CDN dependencies, and the `@media print` pagination logic.

The "page through it once" need is served by this skill's own `scripts/preview-page.mjs` (which generates `preview/play/index.html`),
and that code has nothing to do with upstream: the upstream runtime.js is built for a live human presentation (presenter window, teleprompter script, timer, BroadcastChannel two-window sync),
whereas this skill produces a video and has no live speaking step; the play page only replays slide by slide + compares the motion switch + shows the voiceover panel.
